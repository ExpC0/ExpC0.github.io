About Me

I am a Research & Development Engineer at Shanghai BDCOM Information Technology Co., Ltd., where I develop and maintain firmware for industrial Ethernet switches and routers running on the VxWorks RTOS and embedded Linux. My work sits in the networking control plane — most notably the DHCPv4 and DHCPv6 modules, where I have implemented client, server and relay functionality against the relevant IETF RFC specifications for IPv4, IPv6, DHCPv4 and DHCPv6. Day to day I write Embedded C, working with socket programming, operating-system concepts, UBUS and IPC, and I build CLI-to-backend command handlers using KLISH.

I hold a Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science and Engineering from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET). I enjoy the low-level end of the stack — protocols, IPC, and getting reliable behaviour out of constrained systems. Alongside my professional work I keep my fundamentals sharp through competitive programming, having solved 500+ problems across Codeforces, LeetCode, HackerRank and AtCoder, and I publish small open-source systems and networking projects in C.
