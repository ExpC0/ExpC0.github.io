01. About

I'm a research-and-development engineer at Shanghai BDCOM (part of FS), where I develop and maintain firmware for network switches and routers on both the VxWorks RTOS and embedded Linux. My work centers on the control plane — most recently contributing to DHCPv4 and DHCPv6 implementations across VxWorks and Linux platforms.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from the Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET). I enjoy the low-level end of the stack: protocols, IPC, and getting reliable behaviour out of constrained systems. Outside of firmware I keep my fundamentals sharp with competitive programming and small open-source systems projects.
