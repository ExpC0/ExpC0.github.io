About Me

I am a Software Engineer who enjoys building complete products across mobile, web, backend and systems software. I have shipped a multi-organization office platform in Flutter and Firebase, a secure full-stack productivity app in Next.js and PostgreSQL, and software ranging from iOS applications to network services in C. I care about thoughtful interfaces, clear architecture, reliable state management and security that is enforced beyond the UI.

My professional background includes working as a Software Engineer (R&D) at Shanghai BDCOM Information Technology Co., Ltd., where I developed networking software for industrial switches and routers running VxWorks and embedded Linux. That experience sharpened my approach to protocols, concurrency, debugging and production reliability; it complements my broader application-engineering work rather than limiting it.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from KUET and have solved 500+ competitive programming problems, keeping my data structures, algorithms and problem-solving fundamentals strong.
